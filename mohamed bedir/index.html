About Me

Language. Lens.
Leadership.

I am a dedicated English language educator with deep roots in both the classroom and the creative industry. My teaching journey began at the primary level, nurturing young minds with a love for language — and evolved into shaping the next generation of English graduates at the Faculty of Arts.

Beyond the classroom, I wear multiple hats: photographer, video editor, director, and scriptwriter. I believe language and visual storytelling are two sides of the same coin — both demand precision, creativity, and a deep understanding of the audience.

My advertising scripts have helped brands communicate their vision, while my editing work has brought countless stories to life through rhythm, color, and emotion.
