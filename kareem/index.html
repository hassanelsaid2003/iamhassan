About Me

Hey! I'm Kareem Al-Gamal, a Computer and Communications Engineering student at the Faculty of Engineering, Mansoura University. I'm passionate about technology and always curious about how things work — especially when it comes to networking, cybersecurity, and building smart systems.

My journey in tech started with learning web development through HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, but over time my interest shifted more toward Networking and Cybersecurity — I've been building my knowledge in network fundamentals, Linux environments, ethical hacking concepts, and problem-solving through CTF (Capture the Flag) challenges.

On the other side, I have a deep passion for Robotics Design. I love combining hardware and software — building machines that move, sense, and respond to the world. Whether it's prototyping with Arduino or designing robot structures, I find it incredibly exciting.
